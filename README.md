A newspaper front page — "WORLD TODAY" — summing up the world news of Thursday, 19 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image. The lead story is "THE SUMMIT OF THREE": Trump, Xi and Putin meeting face to face in Shenzhen for the first trilateral summit of the three presidents, illustrated with a navy graphic band showing a night skyline, a gold sun and three overlapping circles for the three leaders at Kylin Villa. Around it sit six short stories — nuclear arms talks, the IEA's darkest energy warning, Israeli tanks crossing the ceasefire line in Gaza, Europe watching from Berlin, the expiring US–China trade truce, and Tehran's rejection of US missile claims — plus a footer ticker of the day's other headlines. The image is generated deterministically by `artwork.py` using Pillow.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.png
```

Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write to a different directory. To regenerate the artwork itself, run `python3 artwork.py`.
